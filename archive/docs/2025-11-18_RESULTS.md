---

## Key Observation

**Higher degree nodes have dramatically more paths**, confirming the relationship shown in Himmelstein et al. (2023) Figure 4:

- Path counts increase with both source and target degree
- Pattern preserved in permuted networks (degree distribution maintained)
- Biological structure in unpermuted adds specific patterns beyond pure degree effects

This explains why:
1. DWPC values increase with degree (Himmelstein's finding)
2. Our models achieve r≈0.91 at optimal path lengths (degree signal strong)
3. Edge probabilities decrease with degree but path counts increase (combinatorics)
